How it works
01

Describe or upload

Type room dimensions and style, or attach a floor plan image. Plana reads door arcs, window lines, and wall measurements from your photo.

02A2UI

AI generates the layout

Every piece of furniture is placed with real centimetre dimensions. The layout streams into the canvas live via A2UI declarative envelopes — no hallucinated sizes.

03AG-UI

Drag, rotate, refine

Move furniture freely — collision detection prevents overlaps and door blockage. The agent watches moves via AG-UI and comments on flow and clearance in real time.

Features
Core

Pixel-perfect to scale

Every furniture piece has real centimetre dimensions. No guessing, no generic stock sizes. The agent places everything precisely from your room measurements.

Collision detection

Pieces can’t overlap or block door arcs. Rugs layer underneath collidable furniture.

Real-time

One-click rotate

Hit rotate. Dimensions update instantly — the agent tracks the new footprint automatically.

90° increments

Any room, any furniture

Pianos, aquariums, custom hex colours. The AI names and sizes whatever you describe.
